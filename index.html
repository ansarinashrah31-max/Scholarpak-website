About Us

Empowering Pakistani Girls Through Education

ScholarPak is dedicated to helping Pakistani girls access quality education through 100% free, verified, and accessible scholarships. We partner with universities, NGOs, and donors to provide financial support, mentorship, and guidance to talented girls across Pakistan. Our mission is to remove financial barriers and create opportunities.
